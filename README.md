## 🧭 A little about me

I'm an undergraduate student and developer who enjoys turning ideas into practical software. I like understanding what happens beneath an interface—and making the interface itself feel clear and thoughtful.

My interests span desktop, mobile, and web development. I learn by building, experimenting, and refining, whether that means exploring a new language or finding a better way to handle an everyday task.

## 🧠 What I'm exploring

- **Artificial intelligence** — How AI works and where it can be useful in real applications.
- **Agentic coding** — Building with AI agents and exploring how they can help develop and improve software.
- **Reverse engineering** — Taking a closer look at existing software to understand its behavior and design.
- **Systems & performance** — How applications interact with hardware and the operating system.
- **Interface design** — Clean layouts, typography, and the small interactions that make tools pleasant to use.
