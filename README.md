## A little about me

I'm a project manager who likes getting close to the work: untangling requirements, setting priorities, and keeping the path to release clear. I care about the decisions that give a project its shape—what belongs in scope, what needs another pass, and what is ready to ship.

I also build desktop, mobile, and web tools. Working with Codex and AGY lets me move between planning, prototyping, and implementation, with a particular interest in interface design, software internals, and the tradeoffs behind a finished app.

## What I'm exploring

- **Artificial intelligence** — How AI works and where it can be useful in real applications.
- **Agentic coding** — Building with AI agents and exploring how they can help develop and improve software.
- **Reverse engineering** — Taking a closer look at existing software to understand its behavior and design.
- **Systems & performance** — How applications interact with hardware and the operating system.
- **Interface design** — Clean layouts, typography, and the small interactions that make tools pleasant to use.
